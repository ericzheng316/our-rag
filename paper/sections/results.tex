\section{Does Coverage Improve Deep Search?}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{The matched comparison}
\label{sec:results-pair}

Table~\ref{tab:main-pair} reports the matched pair of
\S\ref{sec:setup-comparisons} on the closed-pool MuSiQue development
set: coverage-shaped RL against outcome-only RL from the same SFT
initialization, the same schedule, and the same data order. Overall
exact match improves by $+1.65$ points ($z=2.37$). The gain is
depth-structured: nothing at two hops, $+1.6$ at three, and $+7.2$
at four hops ($z=3.98$; 41 questions flip to correct against 12 that
flip to wrong). Partitioning the dev set by retrieval difficulty
instead of hop count gives the same shape: $-2.2$ on the easy third,
$+1.8$ on the medium, $+5.3$ on the hard third ($z=2.72$). The
improvement lands where \S\ref{sec:starvation} locates the starved
groups.

The shaped policy also searches less at every depth: 4.70 versus 4.98
calls per question overall, 6.21 versus 6.56 on 4-hop questions.

\subsection{Consistency across seeds}
\label{sec:results-seeds}

Replicating the shaped recipe under explicit seeds 1--3
(\S\ref{sec:setup-model}) gives 47.12, 47.83, and 48.49 overall EM
($47.8 \pm 0.7$), with 4-hop EM 34.6, 33.3, and 38.0. A separate
batch of same-seed reruns, which fixes the data order and varies only
training stochasticity, spans $47.5 \pm 1.6$ (Appendix~D). Extended
training past the fixed 100-episode endpoint
reaches 50.27 EM at episode 120 and degrades beyond it
(Appendix~E).

\subsection{The same-base ladder}
\label{sec:results-ladder}

Table~\ref{tab:ladder} places the result on the ladder of the same
base model. Closed-book answering reaches 4.8 EM; a single retrieval
15.4; the search protocol alone, zero-shot, 29.0 with a 13.5\%
invalid-output rate; SFT 34.5; outcome-only RL adds twelve points;
coverage shaping adds 1.65 on top. At four hops the ladder runs
$1.7 \rightarrow 7.9 \rightarrow 10.1 \rightarrow 24.9 \rightarrow
30.6 \rightarrow 37.8$: the two RL rungs contribute most of the
deep-hop capability, and the shaping term accounts for 7.2 points of
it.
