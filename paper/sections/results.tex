\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\textbf{Policy and training.} The policy is Qwen3.5-9B~\citep{yang2025qwen3}
with a LoRA adapter~\citep{hu2021lora}, initialized by supervised
fine-tuning on protocol-formatted trajectories generated by a 122B
teacher over MuSiQue training questions; the teacher is used only for
SFT data. Each RL run performs 100 episodes of 32 questions with $K=8$
rollouts per question (temperature 0.7, at most 8 turns) under explicit
seeds that fix the data order, initialization, and sampler. Three
variants share each seed: \emph{query-level credit} ($M=3$,
$\beta_q=1$), the \emph{trajectory-level coverage} term ($\beta=0.3$,
Appendix~\ref{app:traj}), and \emph{outcome-only} training.

\textbf{Environments.} Training uses MuSiQue-Ans~\citep{trived2022musique}
in its closed-pool form: each question ships 20 candidate paragraphs,
and a search ranks them by token-level F1 against the query and returns
the top three. Evaluation uses the closed pool on the full MuSiQue
development set (2{,}417 questions; 1{,}252/760/405 at 2/3/4 hops), and
dense retrieval (e5-base-v2~\citep{wang2024text,Jin2025flashrag}) over
wiki18 (21M passages) and over the 139k-paragraph union of the MuSiQue
pools; HotpotQA~\citep{yang2018hotpotqa} and
2WikiMultihopQA~\citep{ho2020constructing} are evaluated zero-shot on
2{,}000-question subsets. Continued training against the open retriever
starts from a step-100 checkpoint and trains 50 further episodes with
wiki18 retrieval in the loop.

\textbf{Metrics.} Alias-aware exact match and token-level F1 with
2/3/4-hop breakdowns, search calls per question, and final coverage
(Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage}, averaged over questions). Two systems evaluated
on the same questions are compared with the paired McNemar test, and
$z$ is reported. All numbers use the fixed final checkpoint (step 100;
step 50 for continued training). Full details in
Appendix~\ref{app:setup}.

\subsection{Breaking answer ties}
\label{sec:results-main}
\label{sec:mech-broken}

Figure~\ref{fig:variants} compares query-level credit with outcome-only
training on the closed-pool MuSiQue development set (per-hop values,
F1, final coverage, and search calls in Table~\ref{tab:main-qc},
Appendix~\ref{app:main-table}). Query-level credit reaches 51.43 exact
match against 47.62 ($+3.8$; 236 questions flip to correct against 144,
$z=4.72$), and the gain grows with depth: $+2.5$ at two hops, $+3.3$ at
three, and $+8.9$ at four ($z=4.08$; 57:21). It reaches more of the
gold set (final coverage 83.0\% vs.\ 81.2\%) with fewer search calls
(4.31 vs.\ 4.86 per question), and it answers correctly more often both
when its evidence is complete (43.4\% of questions vs.\ 41.0\%) and when
it is not (7.9\% vs.\ 6.5\%). Outcome-only training reaches 47.62, 47.50,
and 47.62 EM over seeds 1--3, so the gain is five times the seed spread.
Along training the query-level run leads at every evaluated checkpoint:
46.92 at step 20 (outcome-only 44.52), 51.68 at step 50 (46.88 over
three seeds), 52.50 at step 80 (49.03), and 51.43 at step 100, while
searching 0.4--0.6 fewer times per question throughout.

Table~\ref{tab:k8-variants} audits the query-level and outcome-only variants
of seed 1 at step 100 with the same protocol. On the same 799
questions, the query-level variant has fewer all-wrong groups (32.5\% vs.\
35.5\%; at four hops 37.3\% vs.\ 42.9\%, 31 groups resolved against 13
lost, $z=2.71$) and more all-correct groups (19.4\% vs.\ 14.9\%; at four
hops 12.5\% vs.\ 7.8\%). Sampled accuracy is higher (pass@1 42.5 vs.\
38.1; at four hops 36.9 vs.\ 30.2) and so is pass@8 (67.5 vs.\ 64.5; at
four hops 62.7 vs.\ 57.1). The coverage variation inside tied groups
and its within-group AUC are the same for both variants (4-hop groups with
coverage variation 83.6 vs.\ 81.5\%; AUC 0.85 vs.\ 0.85): the signal
itself does not change with training; what changes is that the
query-level variant has used it. The ties that disappear are the
problem ties, and the ties that appear are solved ones.
How the credit reshapes the groups the estimator learns from is the
subject of \S\ref{sec:mechanism}.

\subsection{Open corpus and comparison with search-RL systems}
\label{sec:results-open}
\label{sec:results-stage2}

Table~\ref{tab:external} evaluates the trained policies under dense
retrieval over wiki18 and over the 139k-paragraph MuSiQue pool union,
against six search-RL systems---R3-RAG~\citep{li2025r3},
ZeroSearch~\citep{sun2026zerosearch},
R1-Searcher~\citep{song2025r1searcher},
StepSearch~\citep{zheng-etal-2025-stepsearch},
Search-R1~\citep{jin2025searchr1}, and
ReSearch~\citep{chen2025research}---reproduced under the same retriever
with their released checkpoints and protocols. With closed-pool training
only, the query-level run already scores above every external system on
wiki18 MuSiQue (26.35 vs.\ 22.09 for ReSearch, $z=5.12$) and matches the
strongest on HotpotQA (45.60 vs.\ 45.00 for Search-R1); its 4-hop lead
carries over (15.31 vs.\ 11.36 for outcome-only training, $z=2.41$),
while its overall closed-pool lead does not, and it abstains far less
(5.2\% of MuSiQue questions vs.\ 8.5\%; full comparison in
Table~\ref{tab:open-qc}, Appendix~\ref{app:open-tables}). Two
measurements say what has to change. Against wiki18, the official
sub-questions with gold intermediate answers substituted in reach the
complete gold set of 43.8/24.9/15.6\% of MuSiQue questions at 2/3/4
hops, and querying by the gold paragraphs' titles reaches it for
80.1/62.1/62.0\% (Table~\ref{tab:reach}, Appendix~\ref{app:open-tables}):
the passages are present and the index returns them when asked by
title; the policy's queries, phrased for the lexical pool retriever, do
not reach them. We therefore continue training from the step-100
checkpoint for 50 episodes with wiki18 retrieval in the loop
(\S\ref{sec:setup}). The continued run reaches 28.55 EM on MuSiQue
($+2.2$ over its checkpoint, $z=3.11$; $+6.5$ over ReSearch, $z=7.80$),
48.55 on HotpotQA ($+3.6$ over Search-R1, $z=3.94$, which is trained on
HotpotQA), and 44.45 on 2WikiMultihopQA, level with ReSearch (44.15,
$z=0.24$) and below R3-RAG (47.65), whose training data include
2WikiMultihopQA; on the 139k corpus, where every
gold paragraph is present, the extended-training checkpoint of
Appendix~\ref{app:extended} scores 45.64 against 32.77.

\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:results-ablation}

\paragraph{Placement of the evidence signal.}
Figure~\ref{fig:paired-seeds} reports the trajectory-level coverage
term against outcome-only training over four matched pairs of runs (same
SFT initialization, schedule, data order, and rollout samples;
explicit seeds 1--3 and the default seed). Pooled over the four pairs,
4-hop exact match improves by $+3.6$ points (157:99, $z=3.62$); pooled
over the three explicit-seed pairs, by $+2.4$ (116:87, $z=2.04$). The
per-pair 4-hop differences are $+7.2$, $+4.2$, $-2.7$, and $+5.7$
(Table~\ref{tab:main-pair}, Appendix~\ref{app:main-table}). Overall EM moves by $+0.9$ ($z=2.36$;
$+0.6$, $z=1.42$, over the explicit-seed pairs), and search calls per
question are unchanged. The term breaks ties at the level of whole
trajectories and buys a deep-hop gain of this size in the training
environment; \S\ref{sec:mech-broadcast} shows what the broadcast does
at the level of turns and what the habit it teaches costs outside the
training environment.

\paragraph{Run-level variation.}
At the run level, the trajectory-level variants of the three explicit-seed
pairs reach 47.12, 47.83, and 48.49 overall EM ($47.8 \pm 0.7$) with
4-hop EM 34.6, 33.3, and 38.0 ($35.3 \pm 2.4$); the outcome-only variants
reach 47.37, 47.54, and 46.67 ($47.2 \pm 0.5$) with 4-hop EM 30.4, 36.1,
and 32.4 ($32.9 \pm 2.9$). A separate batch of same-seed reruns of the
trajectory-level recipe, which fixes the data order and varies only
training stochasticity, spans $47.5 \pm 1.6$ (Appendix~\ref{app:seeds}). Extended
training of that recipe past the fixed 100-episode endpoint reaches
50.27 EM at episode 120 and degrades beyond it (Appendix~\ref{app:extended}); the
query-level variant passes that value at step 50.

\paragraph{What continued training changes.}
From the same outcome-only checkpoint, 50 episodes against wiki18 with
query-level credit reach 28.67 EM on MuSiQue, 47.80 on HotpotQA, and
47.00 on 2WikiMultihopQA, against 28.30, 46.80, and 44.30 without the
credit ($+0.4$, $z=0.61$; $+1.0$, $z=1.80$; $+2.7$, 132:78, $z=3.73$);
the query-level checkpoint continued with credit reaches 28.55, 48.55,
and 44.45. A control that continues the outcome-only checkpoint for 30
episodes in the closed pool instead moves the other way on every
open-corpus set (MuSiQue 26.56, $z=-2.59$; 2Wiki 38.85, $z=-3.41$): the
open-corpus gain comes from the environment, not from additional
training. Continued training against the open retriever lowers
closed-pool EM for every run (to 44.2--46.2 from 47.6--51.4): retrieval
behaviour specializes to the retriever it is trained against, in both
directions (full table in Appendix~\ref{app:open-tables}).
