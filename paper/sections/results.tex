\section{Does Coverage Improve Deep Search?}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Matched pairs}
\label{sec:results-pair}

Table~\ref{tab:main-pair} reports four matched pairs of runs on the
closed-pool MuSiQue development set: coverage-shaped RL against
outcome-only RL from the same SFT initialization, the same schedule,
and the same data order (\S\ref{sec:setup-comparisons}). Three pairs
use explicit seeds 1--3; the fourth is the default-seed pair, which
shares the data order but not the sampler seed. Pooled over the four
pairs, 4-hop exact match improves by $+3.6$ points (157 questions flip
to correct against 99, $z=3.62$); pooled over the three explicit-seed
pairs, by $+2.4$ (116:87, $z=2.04$). The per-pair 4-hop differences
are $+7.2$, $+4.2$, $-2.7$, and $+5.7$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:paired-seeds}).
The effect is depth-structured: pooled over the four pairs, $+0.2$ at
two hops, $+0.6$ at three, and $+3.6$ at four. Overall EM improves by
$+0.9$ ($z=2.36$; $+0.6$, $z=1.42$, over the explicit-seed pairs).
Search calls per question are unchanged: the pooled difference is
$-0.03$ overall and $-0.08$ at four hops, with per-pair differences
between $-0.28$ and $+0.21$. The improvement lands where
\S\ref{sec:starvation} locates the all-wrong groups.

\subsection{Run-level variation}
\label{sec:results-seeds}

At the run level, the shaped arms of the three explicit-seed pairs
reach 47.12, 47.83, and 48.49 overall EM ($47.8 \pm 0.7$) with 4-hop
EM 34.6, 33.3, and 38.0 ($35.3 \pm 2.4$); the outcome-only arms reach
47.37, 47.54, and 46.67 ($47.2 \pm 0.5$) with 4-hop EM 30.4, 36.1, and
32.4 ($32.9 \pm 2.9$). A separate batch of same-seed reruns of the
shaped recipe, which fixes the data order and varies only training
stochasticity, spans $47.5 \pm 1.6$ (Appendix~D). Extended training
past the fixed 100-episode endpoint reaches 50.27 EM at episode 120
and degrades beyond it (Appendix~E).

\subsection{The same-base ladder}
\label{sec:results-ladder}

Table~\ref{tab:ladder} places the result on the ladder of the same
base model. Closed-book answering reaches 4.8 EM; a single retrieval
15.4; the search protocol alone, zero-shot, 29.0 with a 13.5\%
invalid-output rate; SFT 34.5; outcome-only RL adds 12.7 points;
coverage shaping adds 0.6 on top (means over explicit seeds 1--3). At
four hops the ladder runs
$1.7 \rightarrow 7.9 \rightarrow 10.1 \rightarrow 24.9 \rightarrow
32.9 \rightarrow 35.3$: the two RL rungs contribute most of the
deep-hop capability, and the shaping term accounts for 2.4 points of
it on the seed means and 3.6 pooled over the four matched pairs.
