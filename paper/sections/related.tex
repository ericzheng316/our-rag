\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{RL for search agents.} Multi-hop QA systems first
interleaved retrieval with prompted
reasoning~\citep{yao2023react,press2023measuring,trivedi2023interleaving};
the current generation trains the interleaving itself with
reinforcement learning. Search-R1~\citep{jin2025searchr1},
ReSearch~\citep{chen2025research},
R1-Searcher~\citep{song2025r1searcher},
ZeroSearch~\citep{sun2026zerosearch}, and R3-RAG~\citep{li2025r3}
all optimize a terminal answer reward with a group-relative
policy-gradient estimator in the GRPO/RLOO
family~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath,ahmadian2024rloo,kool2019buy}. Our
training loop belongs to this family; our subject is the estimator
itself---what the within-group comparison learns from when the task
is deep, and what it cannot.

\paragraph{Process supervision and path rewards.} Process
supervision scores intermediate steps with a learned reward
model~\citep{lightman2024verify,uesato2022solving}. In search RL,
StepSearch~\citep{zheng-etal-2025-stepsearch} treats the answer
reward as temporally sparse and densifies it: a TF-IDF information
gain toward the gold documents and a redundancy penalty are paid at
the end of every search round and propagated to tokens by a learned
critic under PPO, alongside a query reward against reference queries
synthesized by GPT-4o. Search-P1~\citep{xia2026searchp1} scores
trajectory paths against reference plans generated offline. Both
assign credit to steps. Our term is a property of the whole
trajectory---the fraction of the annotated gold set the rollout
retrieved, credited once per document and only when the rollout
answers---and it enters the leave-one-out advantage as a within-group
ranking (\S\ref{sec:method-effect}). Its job is to order rollouts
that the answer reward cannot order; that failure is measured in
\S\ref{sec:starvation} and analyzed in neither line. StepSearch
reports that group-relative training is unstable under its rewards
and moves to an actor-critic estimator; we keep the group-relative
estimator and repair what it compares.
Classical shaping theory designs reward terms that leave the optimal
policy invariant~\citep{ng1999policy}; the coverage term is designed
to change the within-group ordering, displacing the cost-driven one of
\S\ref{sec:starve-cost}.

\paragraph{Uninformative groups in group-relative RL.} Advantage
collapse---groups whose identical rewards yield zero
advantage---has been named and quantified in mathematical
RLVR~\citep{he2026collapse}, and the existing responses form three
families. \emph{Discard}: dynamic sampling and selective-rollout
methods drop or skip uniform-outcome
groups~\citep{yu2025dapo,greso2025,xu2026downsampling}, recovering
compute at the price of abandoning the hardest questions.
\emph{Synthesize}: virtual reward samples make collapsed groups emit
gradient again~\citep{he2026collapse}; the magnitude returns, but the
ordering within the group remains arbitrary. \emph{Restructure}:
Stratified GRPO re-baselines advantages within structural
strata~\citep{zhu2025grpo}, and Tree-GRPO compares trajectories along
shared tree prefixes~\citep{Ji2026tree}; both repair cross-question
comparability and leave a fully tied group as uninformative as
before. \citet{wang2026darkroom} shows what happens when tied groups
are left to chance: a dense self-prediction reward occupying
all-failure groups drives a GRPO-trained agent into a degenerate
absorbing state. Their amplification runs through z-score
normalization; our estimator is plain leave-one-out, and the lesson
transfers: whatever residual signal occupies an all-failure group can
come to govern the policy, which our cost pathology
(\S\ref{sec:starve-cost}) instantiates with ordinary cost terms. We take a fourth option, \emph{content}: gold evidence
annotations already present in multi-hop training data give tied
groups an ordering that measures progress. Relative to the collapse
literature, our diagnosis also adds what deep search changes: ties
are the dominant regime rather than an occasional efficiency loss,
their character shifts with depth from all-right to all-wrong, and the
residual ordering is a systematic preference for the rollout that
searched least.
