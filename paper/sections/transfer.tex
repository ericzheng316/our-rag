\section{Where Does Coverage Transfer?}
\label{sec:transfer}

Coverage shaping is trained in a closed pool, where every gold document
is reachable by construction. What survives outside? We evaluate the
default-seed matched pair of Table~\ref{tab:main-pair} (coverage-shaped
versus outcome-only, identical schedule) zero-shot under the unified
open-corpus retrieval environment, on open-corpus MuSiQue, HotpotQA, and
2WikiMultihopQA.

\subsection{Transfer is structure-dependent}
\label{sec:transfer-results}

Table~\ref{tab:open-paired} reports the paired comparison. The effect
transfers selectively. On open MuSiQue the closed-pool gains attenuate to
non-significance (overall $z=-1.41$; the 4-hop gain of
\S\ref{sec:results} goes to $z=+0.26$). On HotpotQA the aggregate is
flat, with a negative trend on bridge questions. The effect survives,
significantly, in two places: HotpotQA \emph{comparison} questions
($+4.75$, $z=+3.80$) and 2WikiMultihopQA overall ($+2.05$, $z=+3.01$;
4-hop $+4.52$, $z=+2.39$)---the structures whose questions require
assembling several parallel evidence branches, where completeness of
the evidence set is the binding skill. Two further observations:
outcome-only RL beats SFT on the open corpus ($z=+2.49$), so the
open-corpus improvement from RL does not depend on shaping; and the
shaped policy reaches parity on open MuSiQue while searching about 7\%
less (3.47 vs.\ 3.75 calls per question).

\subsection{The boundary is reachability, measured}
\label{sec:transfer-reach}

Why does the deep-hop effect, causal in the closed pool, go to zero on
open MuSiQue? Decompose success as
\[
P(\text{correct}) \;=\; P(\text{evidence reachable})\times
P(\text{correct}\mid\text{reachable}),
\]
and measure the first factor directly against the open corpus.
Table~\ref{tab:reach} reports gold-title coverage of top-10 retrieval
under two query regimes: the question itself (naive), and an
\emph{oracle} decomposition using the official sub-questions with gold
intermediate answers substituted in---an upper bound no policy can
exceed.\footnote{Title matching across corpus versions is exact, so all
reachability numbers are lower bounds.} At four hops, naive queries
reach the complete gold set for 0.2\% of questions; even oracle
sub-queries reach it for only 15.6\%. In the training pool, that number
is 100\% by construction. The skill coverage shaping trains---finish
the evidence chain before answering---has no purchase on questions
where finishing the chain is physically impossible for the retriever,
which at four hops is more than 84\% of them. Consistently, the shaped
policy's open-corpus 4-hop accuracy (11.1\%) already sits near the
oracle full-evidence ceiling. Coverage rewards repair a discrimination
problem; on open MuSiQue the binding problem is reachability, and no
trajectory-ranking signal can repair that. Where structure keeps
evidence reachable---2Wiki's parallel branches, Hotpot
comparisons---the discipline continues to pay
(Table~\ref{tab:open-paired}).
