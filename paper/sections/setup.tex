\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Policy, initialization, and training}
\label{sec:setup-model}

The policy is Qwen3.5-9B~\citep{yang2025qwen3} with a LoRA
adapter~\citep{hu2021lora}; rollouts are served by
vLLM~\citep{Kwon2023memory} and updates are applied to a separate training
copy. Before RL, the policy is initialized by supervised fine-tuning on
protocol-formatted trajectories. The SFT trajectories were generated by
a 122B teacher
model over MuSiQue training questions, with plans anchored to the
official question decompositions, then filtered for protocol compliance
and leakage. The teacher appears \emph{only} here; reinforcement
learning, evaluation, and deployment use the 9B policy alone, and the
causal comparisons in \S\ref{sec:results} hold the SFT initialization
fixed across arms, so teacher quality does not confound them.

Each training run performs 100 episodes with 32 questions per episode
and $K=8$ rollouts per question (sampling temperature 0.7, at most 8
turns). A run visits 3{,}200 of the 19{,}938 available
training questions, each at most once. Runs use explicit random
seeds that control the data order, parameter initialization, and the
rollout sampler, so that a shaped run and its outcome-only counterpart
with the same seed see identical question sequences and differ only
through the reward. Remaining hyperparameters are listed in
Appendix~C.

\subsection{Environments and datasets}
\label{sec:setup-data}

\textbf{Training} uses MuSiQue-Ans~\citep{trived2022musique} in its
closed-pool form: each question ships an official pool of 20 candidate
paragraphs (gold supporting paragraphs plus curated distractors), and
the agent's searches retrieve from this pool. No live retriever is
needed during training. \textbf{Evaluation} uses two regimes.
\emph{Closed-pool} evaluation
mirrors the training environment on the full MuSiQue development set
(2{,}417 questions; 1{,}252 two-hop, 760 three-hop, 405 four-hop).
\emph{Open-corpus} evaluation replaces the pool with retrieval over a
21M-passage Wikipedia corpus served by an e5-base-v2 dense
index~\citep{wang2024text,Jin2025flashrag}, shared across every system we
compare. Zero-shot transfer is evaluated on
HotpotQA~\citep{yang2018hotpotqa} and
2WikiMultihopQA~\citep{ho2020constructing} without any training on either.

\subsection{Comparisons}
\label{sec:setup-comparisons}

The primary causal comparison is \emph{outcome-only RL versus
coverage-shaped RL}: identical SFT initialization, identical training
budget and schedule, identical evaluation protocol, and matched explicit
seeds, so that each shaped run pairs with an outcome-only run at the
run level. Secondary comparisons position the system: the zero-shot
base policy, SFT-only, and external search-RL systems reproduced under
the unified open-corpus environment (\S\ref{sec:results}; model-scale
and training-data caveats are stated with the table).

\subsection{Metrics and statistics}
\label{sec:setup-metrics}

We report alias-aware exact match (SQuAD normalization plus official
answer aliases) and token-level F1, with 2/3/4-hop breakdowns, search
calls per question, and invalid/give-up rates. Deep-hop (4-hop) exact
match is the primary endpoint of the causal claim
(\S\ref{sec:starvation}); overall EM is always reported alongside.
Statistical reporting separates two sources of uncertainty. Across
questions, we use question-level paired McNemar tests within each
matched pair of runs. Across training runs, we report per-seed paired
differences individually. A separate batch of
same-seed reruns, which quantifies training stochasticity at a fixed
data order, is reported in Appendix~D and never pooled with the
explicit-seed batch.

\subsection{Checkpoint policy}
\label{sec:setup-checkpoint}

All primary numbers use the fixed final checkpoint of the 100-episode
schedule for every arm; the extended-training sweep appears in
Appendix~E.
